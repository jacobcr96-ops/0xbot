## 1. BONK — Solana community airdrop revival

**Name/ticker:** Bonk / BONK  
**Chain:** Solana  
**CA:** `DezXAZ8z7PnrnRJjz3wXBoRgixCa6xjnB7YaB1pPB263` (**HIGH**)

### T0 definition
- **Preferred T0:** first liquid DEX / CEX-accessible trading window — widely reported as **~2022-12-30 UTC** (trading began after Christmas airdrop announcement).  
- Alternate: airdrop announcement / claim window **2022-12-25** (not fully “tradeable” for everyone yet).  
- **Confidence:** MED–HIGH on date; exact minute of first Raydium/Orca fill is **LOW** without archival DEX logs.

### Timeline of observable info (anti-hindsight)

| Horizon | What a desk could see | Conf. |
|---------|----------------------|-------|
| **T0** | Dog-themed Solana meme framed as “for the people” post-FTX; X account `@bonk_inu`; one-pager describing **50% supply airdrop** to NFT holders / traders / artists / devs (~297k NFTs cited later); SOL depressed (~$8 context widely discussed); no VC narrative; thin digital footprint beyond Twitter | HIGH narrative / MED metrics |
| **T0+15m / +1h** | Exact MC/liq/vol at these slices **not reliably recovered** from public writeups; expect chaotic airdrop claim + first DEX fills | LOW |
| **T0+6h / +24h** | Rapid CT buzz; Orca later cited ~$20M volume around airdrop period; listings expanding Dec 30 onward | MED |
| **Week 1** | Price return path later measured ~300%+ in ~8 days (CoinGecko research) — **that is post-hoc**, but volume/attention acceleration was contemporaneously visible | MED |

### Outcome (hindsight, labeled)
- First major peak ~**$205M MC** on **2023-01-05**; then sharp drawdown to ~$62–82M within days. Later cycle ATHs much higher (2024).  
- **Class:** Monster runner (ecosystem catalyst), not a rug.

### Candidate early signals (desk without future knowledge)
1. **Broad fair-ish distribution** (large airdrop %) reducing single-wallet dump optics vs VC allocations.  
2. **Chain-level narrative vacuum fill** (SOL post-FTX despair → “people’s coin”).  
3. **Native CT/NFT graph activation** (pre-built Solana NFT holder set).  
4. Rapid secondary listing / DEX volume appearing without celebrity endorsement.

### Confounders / why lookalikes die
- Most “community airdrop dog coins” on weak chains never get DEX depth or CT density.  
- Airdrop recipients dump; without organic new buyers, MC collapses in days.  
- Post-hoc “BONK saved Solana” is hindsight — many concurrent SOL short-squeeze narratives existed.

## Cross-case notes for the intelligence layer

### What was often observable early (testable)
- LP burn / mint+freeze revoke status  
- Holder growth velocity (when indexed)  
- CA spread velocity on X (needs Scout logs — **not fully reconstructed here**)  
- Clustered funding of first buyers (Flow Desk)  
- Pre-existing meme IP vs invented ticker

### What was rarely recoverable at T0+15m from public web alone
- Exact MC/liq/vol/holders snapshots (DexScreener historical API / Birdeye / Wayback gaps)  
- Authentic vs botted X engagement  
- Silent insider inventories disguised as many wallets  

### Explicit anti-hindsight warnings
- Do not use ATH multiple as a feature of the entry decision.  
- Do not treat “became Base mascot” or “Sphere on Vegas” as T0 knowledge.  
- SLERF and LIBRA both produced enormous early volume for opposite reasons — **volume ≠ quality**.
